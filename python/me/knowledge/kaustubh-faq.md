## What is your experience with AI and agents?

My main work is multi-agent systems and the infrastructure that keeps them

My agent orchestration platform runs specialised agents for weeks at a time,
coordinating through a Postgres state machine rather than agent-to-agent chat.
I've also built retrieval systems, including the RAG assistant answering
questions on this site.

I'm working through The Complete Agentic AI Engineering Course (2025) on Udemy.
